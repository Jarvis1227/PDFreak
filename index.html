GOOD TO KNOW

A few small details.
No big surprises.

Do my files leave my device?

No. The PDF is read and the Word file is created entirely in this browser tab. We don't upload or store your document. Closing the tab clears it from the converter.

What happens to the formatting?

Editable text mode preserves text, approximate font sizing, and page boundaries. It does not recreate images, tables, or complex multi-column layouts. Exact appearance mode renders pages as images inside Word, preserving the look but making the words uneditable.

Can I convert a scanned PDF?

Use exact appearance mode to put scanned pages into Word as images. This version does not perform OCR, so it cannot turn a scan into editable text. Pages without a text layer are flagged before an editable export finishes.

What about password-protected PDFs?

If the PDF needs a password to open, we'll ask for it. The password is only used in your current browser session. Conversion respects the PDF's content extraction permissions.
